Milk rate & margins

Milk rate
Used when a milk entry has no price — e.g. society / cooperative rate.
Milking times
Milk entries are recorded against the morning or evening session.
Margin alert threshold
30 %
Checked weekly. Margin = milk income minus all farm input costs and expenses.
How it works
Example month · 100 L a day at ₹38 / L
Milk income₹1,14,000
Less: inputs & expenses
Feed & fodder₹60,000
Labour wages₹15,000
Vet & medicine₹4,000
Electricity & water₹3,000
Other expenses₹3,000
Total expenses₹85,000
Margin₹29,000 · 25 %
25 % is below your 30 % threshold, so you'd get a margin alert that week.
